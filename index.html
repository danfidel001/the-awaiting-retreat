About the
Awaiting Retreat

The Awaiting Retreat 2026 continues the legacy of CMAN's annual spiritual gatherings dedicated to consecration, transformation, and deeper intimacy with God. Hosted once again at Camp Hope in British Columbia, this edition centers on The New, a call to spiritual renewal and divine repositioning as revealed in Luke 5:36–39.

Over the course of three days, participants will engage in worship, prayer, teaching, and personal devotion designed to realign hearts with the leading of the Holy Spirit. Whether attending for the first time or returning from previous editions, participants can expect a spiritually immersive environment marked by clarity, renewal, and life-changing encounters.

Frequently Asked
Questions

Who can attend the retreat? +
The Awaiting Retreat is open to all believers across all nations who desire a deeper encounter with God through prayer, fasting, and worship.
What kind of sessions will be held? +
Sessions include worship, teaching of the Word, prayer, personal devotion times, and spiritual impartation sessions.
What time can I arrive on Thursday? +
Arrival on Thursday is between 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM. Please plan accordingly.
Is there any age restriction for attendees? +
The retreat is open to adults 18 years and above. Special arrangements may be made for families.
I cannot make it on Thursday. Can I still attend if I arrive on Friday? +
Yes, you may arrive on Friday, though we recommend arriving Thursday for the complete experience.
Are married couples allowed to stay together at the camp? +
Accommodation arrangements vary by location. Contact the organizing team for specific camp details.
Are phones allowed during the retreat? +
We encourage minimal phone usage to maximize your spiritual experience. Specific guidelines will be shared.
What does the registration fee include? +
The registration fee covers accommodation, meals, and access to all retreat sessions and materials.
What should I bring to the retreat? +
Bring your Bible, notebook, comfortable clothing, personal toiletries, and a heart ready for encounter.
Will food and water be provided during the retreat? +
Yes, meals and water will be provided as part of your registration. Dietary needs can be communicated in advance.

Support The Awaiting Retreat

We recognize that not everyone who desires to attend The Awaiting Retreat may have the financial means to do so. If you are in a position to assist, your contribution can help cover the cost of accommodation for someone else.
